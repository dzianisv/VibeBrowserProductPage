Your AI agent is smart. But it cannot see your mail, your calendar, or any page where you are logged in. It only sees the public web.

Vibe fixes that. You paste one URL into your agent, and the agent can use your real Chrome — your tabs, your logins, your pages. You watch it work in your own browser.

We wrote one short guide per agent. Plain words, no code.

## Claude

Open Claude settings, add a custom connector, paste your Vibe URL. That is all. It works on the web, in Cowork, and on mobile.

Guide: [vibebrowser.app/Claude](https://vibebrowser.app/Claude)

## Grok and Grok Bots

Open grok.com/connectors, add a new custom connector, paste the same URL. We wrote the steps from xAI's own docs, and our live test is in progress — the guide says so, in plain sight.

Guide: [vibebrowser.app/GrokBots](https://vibebrowser.app/GrokBots)

## Dots

Dots is new, and we will be honest: we have not tested it yet, and OpenAI has not published how it connects. Reports say Dots uses the same apps as ChatGPT. The ChatGPT setup works today, and our Dots page tracks the check. When it is confirmed, the real steps land there first.

Status page: [vibebrowser.app/Dots](https://vibebrowser.app/Dots)

## Muse (Meta AI)

Open Muse, send it one prompt, and paste your session ID on the secure card — Muse adds Vibe as a custom connector itself. After that it lives in Settings \u2192 Connectors \u2192 Custom connectors, and Muse can use your real Chrome.

Guide: [vibebrowser.app/integrations/muse-connector](https://www.vibebrowser.app/integrations/muse-connector)

## One rule for all of them

Your Vibe URL is a password for your browser. Anyone who has it can drive your logged-in Chrome. Do not post it, do not send it in a chat, and do not put it in a screenshot. If it leaks, open the extension settings and make a new one — the old one stops working at once.

That is the whole story: pick your agent, paste one URL, and let it work in the browser you already trust.
